01 / RESEARCH MISSION

Intelligence needs infrastructure.

As AI agents become increasingly capable of performing complex tasks, a fundamental challenge emerges: how can autonomous systems establish identity, coordinate economic activity and settle payments reliably?

Quoraxis Labs researches open infrastructure for this emerging economic environment. Our approach begins with a focused protocol architecture and develops through implementation, experimentation and reproducible validation.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 01

Agent Identity

Ownership, registration, agent lifecycle management and discoverable identities for autonomous systems.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 02

Economic Coordination

Structured interactions between agents, task assignment, result submission and accountable workflows.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 03

Programmable Settlement

Conditional payment execution, escrow accounting, fund recovery and settlement reliability.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 04

Trust & Verification

Research into result verification, reputation, dispute resolution and economic security.

04 / ACTIVE RESEARCH

What we are working on.

Current and future research tracks supporting the evolution of Quoraxis Protocol.

RESEARCH TRACK / 01 ACTIVE RESEARCH

Settlement Reliability

Investigating mechanisms that reduce the risk of payment deadlocks when one participant becomes unresponsive or refuses to cooperate.

  • Task review deadlines
  • Non-cooperative fund recovery
  • Dispute initiation mechanisms
  • Fair settlement outcomes

Open design challenge

Automatically paying a provider after a deadline may reward invalid submissions. Allowing unilateral refunds may disadvantage legitimate providers.

Quoraxis is exploring settlement designs that address both risks. These mechanisms are not yet implemented in the released protocol.

RESEARCH TRACK / 02 ACTIVE RESEARCH

Economic Security

Expanding the protocol's validation framework to investigate adversarial behavior, access control assumptions and the preservation of user funds.

  • Extended invariant accounting
  • Adversarial state transitions
  • Multi-agent interactions
  • Withdrawal and recovery risks
RESEARCH TRACK / 03 PLANNED RESEARCH

Autonomous Agent Integration

Researching the integration of functional AI agents with programmable task coordination and economic settlement systems.

  • Python agent integration
  • Automated task execution
  • Agent authorization controls
  • Budget and spending limits
RESEARCH TRACK / 04 FUTURE RESEARCH

Trust and Result Verification

Exploring mechanisms through which autonomous systems can evaluate service quality, verify task outcomes and establish economic trust.

  • Agent reputation models
  • Verifiable task results
  • Independent resolution models
  • Interoperability research
05 / OPEN RESEARCH QUESTIONS

The challenges ahead.

Progress requires acknowledging unresolved questions. We publish our current limitations as part of an open research process.

How can autonomous agents verify the quality of work?

A submitted result reference does not establish that the requested task was completed correctly. Reliable verification remains a fundamental research challenge.

How can settlement remain fair without participant cooperation?

Review periods, dispute handling and payment recovery require carefully designed incentives to protect both task requesters and service providers.

How should autonomous spending be controlled?

Agent wallets and automated economic activity require authorization boundaries, spending limits and operational safeguards before real-world use.

How can agent infrastructure remain interoperable?

Future development must examine integration with external AI frameworks, agent communication standards and developer tooling.
