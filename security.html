04 / Open risks & research

What remains unresolved.

Passing tests is evidence about tested behavior, not a guarantee against vulnerabilities.

CURRENT RESEARCH PRIORITY · v0.2.0

Non-cooperative requester and settlement recovery

The expired submitted task test demonstrated at least one requester-initiated settlement or refund path. It does not establish unilateral recovery for the provider if the requester refuses to cooperate.

Review deadlines and dispute resolution need careful design: automatic payment may reward invalid submissions, while unilateral refunds may disadvantage legitimate providers.

  • Define review and dispute state transitions.
  • Test recovery under unresponsive or adversarial participants.
  • Document resolution authority and incentives before release.
Explore research priorities →

Test model limitations

Bounded randomized tests are not exhaustive proofs. Expand adversarial scenarios, forced ETH accounting, multi-agent interactions and deadline boundaries. Review handler guards and revert handling.

Result verification

A submitted result URI does not prove the quality or correctness of work. Verification and fair dispute handling remain research objectives.
