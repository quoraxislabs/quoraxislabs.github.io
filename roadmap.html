04 / Economic strategy

Utility first. Token optional.

Quoraxis currently uses ETH in its experimental settlement contracts. The protocol does not require a proprietary token to function.

Commercial validation

Explore managed infrastructure subscriptions, enterprise integrations and marketplace services. Validate customer demand before committing to pricing or revenue models.

Future token research

Evaluate whether a native ecosystem token would provide meaningful utility for incentives, governance or economic security. Any decision requires economic analysis, security review and applicable legal assessment.
