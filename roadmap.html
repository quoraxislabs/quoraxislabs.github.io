03 / Product ecosystem

Build what other protocols leave unresolved.

Instead of reinventing agent payments or identity, our research focuses on verifiable outcomes, accountable spending and recovery when automated work fails.

◇
01 / CORE THESIS

Verified Execution

Problem: a completed payment does not prove a task was delivered to agreed acceptance criteria. Define machine-checkable conditions, verify eligible evidence and coordinate conditional settlement.

◎
02 / FIRST ADD-ON

Quoraxis Evidence

Problem: companies struggle to reconstruct what an agent did and why it was paid. Preserve task terms, delivery hashes, test outcomes and settlement decision records.

⬡
03 / RISK ENGINE

Quoraxis Guard

Problem: autonomous agents may overspend or act outside approved scope. Enforce spending limits, allowed counterparties, risk gates and human review.

↺
04 / RESILIENCE

Quoraxis Recovery

Problem: failed agent jobs can cause duplicate execution, wasted spend and stuck work. Study retry policies, safe re-routing and dispute-aware recovery.

◈
05 / OPTIMIZATION

Quoraxis Intelligence

Problem: low per-call prices can hide poor completion rates. Compare effective task cost, reliability, retries and verified quality.

⌘
06 / LONG TERM

Cloud, Explorer & Marketplace

Potential managed operations, a public activity explorer and a marketplace. Commercial rollout depends on proven developer demand and security readiness.

Proposed research and product concepts only. None of the six offerings above is a launched commercial product or an existing revenue stream.

04 / Strategic differentiation

Pay for evidence-backed outcomes, not just agent activity.

Agent communication, identity and payment are useful foundations. Our proposed differentiation is a task-level accountability layer connecting acceptance criteria, evidence, spending policy, recovery and settlement.

Example / E-commerce

500 product descriptions

An agent delivers structured copy. Tests check required fields, file integrity, language rules and duplicates. Quoraxis would attach the evidence to a conditional settlement workflow. Human review remains necessary for subjective quality.

Technical boundary

Verification is not truth

Hashes prove data consistency, not factual correctness. External validators can fail or collude. Arbitrators may be trusted parties. The proposed product must document verification limits, appeal paths and economic attack surfaces.

2027 validation gate

Target: two autonomous software agents in an end-to-end demo, 100 documented testnet task cycles and at least 10 external pilot developers or teams. These are goals, not reported adoption.

05 / Economic strategy

Utility first. Token optional.

Quoraxis currently uses ETH in experimental escrow contracts. No proprietary token is required or launched. Business models remain hypotheses until pilots validate demand.

Commercial validation

Explore managed infrastructure subscriptions, enterprise integrations and marketplace services. Validate customer demand before committing to pricing or revenue models.

Future token research

Evaluate whether a native ecosystem token would provide meaningful utility for incentives, governance or economic security. Any decision requires economic analysis, security review and applicable legal assessment.
