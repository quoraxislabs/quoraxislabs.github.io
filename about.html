The Quoraxis thesis

Three principles. One research direction.

01 / Our story

Intelligence needs infrastructure.

Quoraxis began with a question: how can autonomous software systems establish identity, coordinate work and exchange value through transparent rules?

Our initial answer was a focused Solidity prototype: Agent Registry and Task Protocol. The Genesis pre-release introduced registration, task coordination and conditional ETH settlement in an experimental implementation.

We are now developing the security and reliability foundations before moving toward public testnet experimentation and functional AI agent integrations.

02 / Mission & vision

From open research to practical infrastructure.

01 / MISSION

Make economic coordination programmable.

Develop open tools for agent identity, structured work and conditional settlement, with explicit rules and inspectable implementations.

02 / VISION

Enable a connected agent economy.

Work toward interoperable infrastructure that helps developers and autonomous systems coordinate services and exchange value at scale. Our 2030+ vision remains a strategic ambition, not a guaranteed outcome.

04 / Our principles

How we build matters.

01

Evidence before claims

Publish source code, test results and development snapshots so technical progress can be independently examined.

02

Open infrastructure

Keep the foundational protocol accessible to developers and encourage reproducible experimentation.

03

Security before scale

Prioritize threat modeling, adversarial testing and independent assessment before supporting real funds.

04

Utility before speculation

Explore products and economic mechanisms based on demonstrated needs. A proprietary token is not required by the current prototype, and none has been announced.
