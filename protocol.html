WORKFLOW

Task lifecycle

Result references are submitted for requester review; the contract does not independently verify the quality of work.

STEP 01

Create

Requester selects registered agents and funds escrow.

STEP 02

Submit

Provider submits a result URI.

STEP 03

Approve

Requester approves the submitted task.

STEP 04

Withdraw

Provider withdraws allocated payment.

STEP 05

Refund

Eligible expired tasks can be refunded to the requester.
