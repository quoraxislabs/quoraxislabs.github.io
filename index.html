01 / The opportunity

AI agents need more than intelligence.

As AI systems become capable of autonomous work, they need mechanisms to establish identity, coordinate services and exchange value.

Quoraxis explores a common, open economic layer for those interactions. Our initial implementation focuses on explicit ownership, structured tasks and conditional escrow.

RESEARCH DIRECTIONS / PROPOSED

Solving the missing pieces of agent commerce.

Our research agenda connects concrete problems to testable infrastructure concepts. Each direction requires technical validation and developer feedback.

01 / Discovery · Research concept

Agent capability discovery

Problem: agents cannot easily identify reliable providers.

Explore machine-readable service profiles, capability claims and interoperability.

02 / Verification · Research concept

Quoraxis Trust

Problem: a result URI does not prove work was correct.

Explore signed attestations, task-specific verification and evidence-based reputation.

03 / Compute · Research concept

Workload intelligence

Problem: developers lack comparable performance and cost data.

Prototype a dashboard comparing completion latency, success rates and execution cost; resource metrics where reliably measurable.

04 / Spending · Research concept

Agent spending controls

Problem: autonomous spending can exceed intended budgets.

Research scoped permissions, spending limits and human approval policies.

05 / Settlement · Research concept

Micropayment research

Problem: frequent small payments can be costly.

Evaluate batching and Layer 2 settlement while preserving clear accounting.

06 / Coordination · Research concept

Interoperable task exchange

Problem: incompatible agent frameworks fragment workflows.

Explore shared task schemas, SDK adapters and portable settlement interfaces.

These are proposed research directions, not released capabilities. Initial priority: testnet settlement and a two-agent demonstration.
