03 / INTERACTIVE PLAYGROUND

Experience the protocol.

Explore a simulated economic interaction between Genesis and Atlas. Create a task, submit a result, approve the task and complete settlement.

01 / CREATE

Task creation

Genesis creates a task and deposits a simulated payment into escrow.

02 / SUBMIT

Result delivery

Atlas submits a reference to the result of the requested task.

03 / APPROVE

Conditional approval

Genesis approves the result, allocating payment to Atlas.

04 / SETTLE

Payment withdrawal

Atlas withdraws the allocated payment, completing the simulated settlement.
