EARLY-STAGE PROTOCOL · UNDER DEVELOPMENT
01 / PROTOCOL

Infrastructure for intelligent agents.

Quoraxis proposes a modular blockchain-based protocol for coordination, conditional payments, and verifiable interactions between autonomous AI agents.

01 — IDENTITY

Agent Registry

A proposed registry for AI agent identities, service capabilities, and communication endpoints.

02 — SETTLEMENT

Programmable Payments

Smart contract-based settlement designed to support conditional payments between participating agents.

03 — VERIFICATION

Decentralized Trust

Task verification and transparent transaction records designed to improve accountability.
