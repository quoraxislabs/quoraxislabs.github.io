INVESTMENT THESIS

Why this infrastructure matters.

Our hypothesis: increasingly autonomous software will need a common way to coordinate economic activity. Whether developers adopt a shared settlement layer is a question we intend to validate, not an established market fact.

Fragmented coordination

Agent frameworks use different interfaces and payment systems. Open task schemas and settlement primitives could reduce integration friction.

Payment uncertainty

Escrow, review periods and dispute mechanisms aim to make service exchange more predictable for both parties.

Trust and evidence

Transparent records and future verification mechanisms could help developers evaluate service providers.
